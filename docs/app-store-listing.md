# App Store listing kit

## Name (30 characters max)

"Workout Tracker" is generic and probably taken. Check availability in App Store Connect; options:

- Workout Tracker: Muscle Map
- Heatmap Fitness
- Muscle Heat: Workout Log
- Fitly Heat

## Subtitle (30 max)

`Muscle heat map & AI coach`

## Promotional text (170 max, editable any time)

`See which muscles you trained on a glowing 3D body, track your lifts and runs, and get a personal plan from your AI coach.`

## Description

Workout Tracker shows you what your training actually did.

LOG ANYTHING
Strength with sets, reps and weights (and last time's weight ready for you). Running, cycling, rowing, swimming, climbing, sport and mobility with distance, pace and session type. Outdoor mode uses GPS for distance, pace and a route map.

SEE YOUR MUSCLES
A 3D hologram body lights up from blue to red for every muscle you trained, then cools down as you recover. Tap any muscle to see what it is. Choose male, female or neutral, and set your age and weight.

TRACK YOUR PROGRESS
Personal records, estimated one-rep max, total volume and pace charts for every exercise and activity.

AI COACH
Ask for a plan: raise your bench, train for a half marathon, get faster at ultimate. Review it, start it, and tick off sessions as you go.

PRIVATE BY DEFAULT
No account needed. Your workouts stay on your iPhone.

OPTIONAL CYCLE TRACKING
For people who have periods: a gentle reminder that it's fine to lift lighter. Stays on your device.

*The AI coach and cycle notes are general information, not medical advice.*

Primary: Health & Fitness. Secondary: Sports.

## Age rating

Answer the questionnaire truthfully. Things to say yes to: health or wellness topics, and an AI chatbot (if asked). Expect 12+ or 17+ depending on how Apple scores the health and AI answers.

## Privacy nutrition label (App Store Connect → App Privacy)

"Data not collected" would be wrong because the coach sends data to a server. Declare these, all **not linked to the user** and **not used for tracking**, purpose **App Functionality**:

| Apple's category | What it is here |
|---|---|
| Health & Fitness → Fitness | Workout summary, records, body stats sent to the coach (only if the user allows) |
| User Content → Other User Content | Messages typed to the coach |
| Identifiers → Device ID | The random per-install ID used for rate limiting |

Do not declare location, health (cycle) or contacts: they never leave the device.
Tracking: **No**.

## Review notes (App Review Information)

```
No login or account is needed: the app opens to a short welcome screen.

AI coach: open the Plans tab and tap "Ask your AI coach". On first use a consent
screen explains that messages are sent to an AI service. Try: "Give me a 4 week
plan to add 5 kg to my bench". The plan appears in the Plans tab; tap it, then
Start this plan, then Start session to log a workout from it.
The coach is served by our server at [your server URL] and will be running
during review.

Outdoor GPS tracking (Workouts tab > + > Cardio > Running > Track outdoors with GPS)
needs a real device and location permission. Location is only used during a
tracked session and stays on the device.

Cycle tracking is optional and off by default (Settings > Health).

The app is a fitness log. It does not diagnose or treat any condition.
```
