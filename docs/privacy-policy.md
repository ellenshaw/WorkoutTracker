## The short version

- There are no accounts. Your workouts, body details, records, plans and cycle log are stored **on your iPhone**.
- If you use the AI coach, your messages are sent to our server and on to an AI service (Anthropic's Claude) to write replies.
- We don't sell your data, show ads, or track you across other apps and websites.

## What stays on your device

Workouts and the exercises, sets, weights, distances, times and notes you enter; GPS routes from outdoor sessions; the age, weight, height and body shape you choose for the 3D model; personal records; training plans; your coach conversation; and, if you turn it on, your cycle log. You can erase all of it in **Settings → Delete all my data**, or by deleting the app.

Location is used only while you track an outdoor session, to measure distance, pace and route. It is not sent to us.

Cycle tracking is optional and off by default. It is never sent to the coach or to us.

## What is sent when you use the AI coach

- The messages you type to the coach.
- If **Share training info with coach** is on (you choose this on first use and can change it in Settings): your age, weight, height, personal records and a summary of your recent workouts. Never your name, email, location, cycle information or gender.
- A random identifier created on your device, used only to limit how often the coach can be used.

This goes to our server, which passes it to Anthropic to generate a reply. Our server does not store your messages. Anthropic processes the data under its own terms and retention rules: [link Anthropic's current privacy and commercial terms]. Don't type anything into the coach you don't want processed this way.

## Health information

The app is a training log and is **not a medical device**. Coach suggestions and cycle notes are general information, not medical advice, and cycle estimates are not a method of contraception. Speak to a doctor about pain, injuries or health conditions.

## Sharing

We don't share your data except with Anthropic as described above, or if the law requires it.

## Children
